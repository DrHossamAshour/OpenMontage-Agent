# Egypt Drone Retail Ad — 50% Off

## Goal
Drive physical visits to any branch in Egypt.

## Core idea
A national-scale cinematic drone journey across recognizably Egyptian environments, descending from iconic aerial views into streets and retail energy, then landing on the fashion offer.

## Duration
~19–20 seconds.

## Format
Primary: 9:16 vertical social master.
Secondary later: 16:9.

## Voice line
"زوروا أي فرع من فروعنا الآن واستمتعوا بخصم 50%."

## Visual route for POC
1. Giza / Cairo aerial.
2. Aswan / Nile aerial.
3. Alexandria landmark.
4. Egyptian countryside.
5. Clothing-store exterior.
6. Fashion rack / retail detail.
7. 50% offer reveal + visit-any-branch CTA.

## Important
This first cut is a proof of concept using temporary licensed/free stock footage.
Replace the temporary storefront and fashion assets with the actual brand branches, logo, colors, and garments for the production master.
