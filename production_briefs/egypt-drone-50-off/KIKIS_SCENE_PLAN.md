# KIKI'S WEAR — Egypt Drone Branch Visit Commercial

## Campaign
KIKI'S WEAR — Branch Visit / 50% Off

## Objective
Drive visits to physical KIKI'S WEAR branches.

## Master Duration
22 seconds.

## Primary Format
9:16 — 1080x1920 — 30fps.

## Brand Direction
Derived from the supplied KIKI'S visual reference:
- hot pink
- sunny yellow
- soft cream / warm off-white
- playful kids-fashion energy
- cheerful summer/lifestyle accents
- clean, polished, premium-but-fun finish

The supplied circular K logo is the primary brand mark for motion/end-card usage.

## Important Claim Rule
The aerial journey may represent multiple Egyptian governorates/regions, but the edit must NOT imply KIKI'S has a branch in every governorate. The CTA remains: visit any existing KIKI'S branch.

## Beat Map
hook → nationwide journey → descent → branch arrival → kids-fashion payoff → offer reveal → CTA

---

## Scene 01 — Egypt From Above
**Time:** 0.0–3.0s

**Subject:** Recognizable Egyptian urban/heritage landscape from high aerial altitude; no people as primary subjects.

**Subject motion:** Cars and small city motion far below.

**Scene:** Golden-hour Egypt; warm sun; clean sky; premium commercial finish.

**Spatial:** Extreme wide aerial; deep foreground-to-horizon depth.

**Camera:** Drone altitude, high angle, 24mm-equivalent wide lens, deep focus, smooth forward glide with gentle descending movement.

**Transition:** Match-motion transition into next aerial.

**Audio:** Soft wind texture + low cinematic pulse beginning.

**Overlay:** None.

---

## Scene 02 — Coastal / Urban Egypt
**Time:** 3.0–5.8s

**Subject:** Mediterranean Egyptian city/coastline visual language, bright and energetic.

**Subject motion:** Traffic and sea movement.

**Scene:** Daylight/high-key; clear water; colorful city energy.

**Spatial:** Wide aerial, horizon visible, city in midground, coast in foreground.

**Camera:** Smooth drone truck-forward with slight arc, maintaining direction of travel from Scene 01.

**Transition:** Fast match cut using road/coastline direction.

**Audio:** Music beat grows; subtle whoosh on transition.

**Overlay:** None.

---

## Scene 03 — Nile / Green Egypt / Upper Egypt
**Time:** 5.8–8.6s

**Subject:** Nile landscape, greenery, agricultural texture or Upper Egyptian river environment.

**Subject motion:** Water, boats or landscape parallax.

**Scene:** Warm natural daylight, saturated but realistic greens and blues.

**Spatial:** Wide oblique aerial; water path becomes leading line toward camera destination.

**Camera:** Descending drone move; controlled speed-ramp at end to increase energy.

**Transition:** Directional whip/motion-blur bridge into city streets.

**Audio:** Music rises; short riser.

**Overlay:** None.

---

## Scene 04 — Descent Into The Street
**Time:** 8.6–12.2s

**Subject:** Modern Egyptian shopping street, lively but clean; families and pedestrians may appear naturally.

**Subject motion:** People walking, vehicles moving slowly, retail activity.

**Scene:** Bright daytime; family-friendly environment; no readable fake store signage.

**Spatial:** Starts wide above street, descends to near-rooftop/eye-level corridor.

**Camera:** Continuous crane/drone-style descent → forward tracking toward retail frontage. 28–35mm lens equivalent, stabilized.

**Transition:** Hidden cut using a foreground object, pole, wall, tree, or vehicle wipe.

**Audio:** Street ambience enters subtly under music.

**Overlay:** KIKI'S circular logo may appear very briefly as a small brand stinger only in post; do not generate it inside AI footage.

---

## Scene 05 — KIKI'S Branch Arrival
**Time:** 12.2–15.2s

**Subject:** Real KIKI'S WEAR storefront / branch exterior. This shot should ultimately use real branch footage or a controlled composited storefront plate.

**Subject motion:** Doors opening, family/child entering, subtle clothing display movement.

**Scene:** Bright welcoming retail exterior, warm cream + pink/yellow brand accents.

**Spatial:** Medium-wide to medium storefront framing.

**Camera:** Smooth dolly-in / push-in toward entrance, ending centered on doorway/logo area.

**Transition:** Match push through doorway into interior fashion shot.

**Audio:** Soft impact when storefront lands; music becomes more playful.

**Overlay:** Real KIKI'S logo composited in post only if needed.

---

## Scene 06 — Kidswear Fashion Energy
**Time:** 15.2–18.3s

**Subject:** Stylish child model(s), age-appropriate kidswear, cheerful confident movement. No adult-fashion styling.

**Subject motion:** One child walks two to three steps, turns naturally, playful smile; quick cut to rack/detail/texture.

**Scene:** Bright premium kidswear store or clean summer-fashion environment.

**Spatial:** Full-body → medium detail → clothing close-up.

**Camera:** 35–50mm equivalent; shallow-to-medium DoF; smooth tracking; one restrained speed ramp between fashion beats.

**Color:** Hot pink/yellow accents echo supplied campaign artwork without overwhelming skin tones.

**Audio:** Beat peaks; playful pop accent / clap / soft whoosh.

**Overlay:** None.

## Transition Vocabulary
Use only:
- match-motion cut
- restrained whip bridge
- hidden foreground wipe
- hard cut on beat
- short dissolve only if required

Avoid random transition packs.

## Grade
- high-key commercial base
- warm skin tones
- soft cream highlights
- controlled hot pink + sunny yellow brand accents
- clean blacks, no crushed shadows
- no heavy cinematic teal/orange treatment

## Text Policy
Do NOT ask video generation models to create readable Arabic text, discount text, or the logo.
All text, discount numerals, CTA, and logos are added in composition/post.
