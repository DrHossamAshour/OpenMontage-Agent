# Master Multi-Shot Prompt — Seedance / long structured models

Create a 22-second premium KIKI'S WEAR kids-fashion commercial set in Egypt. The emotional arc is nationwide energy → destination → playful fashion payoff → retail offer. Begin with a high-altitude cinematic drone glide over a recognizable Egyptian landscape at warm golden hour. Preserve a consistent forward travel direction. Accelerate gently as the camera descends from aerial height into a modern Egyptian shopping street in bright family-friendly daylight. Use a foreground wipe to hide the transition into a welcoming kidswear storefront arrival. Push smoothly through the entrance into a bright premium children’s fashion environment using soft cream, hot pink and sunny yellow accents inspired by KIKI'S WEAR campaign identity.

Finish on a clean fashion background with deliberate central negative space for a discount graphic added later in post.

Camera language: 24–28mm aerial wides, controlled physical drone descent, 35mm storefront dolly-in, 40–50mm fashion tracking and detail shots, stable horizon, restrained speed ramp only at the transition from Nile to street.

Audio direction: upbeat modern family-fashion rhythm, subtle wind at aerial opening, light urban ambience during street descent, playful pop accents in the fashion section, strong friendly impact at offer reveal. Do not generate spoken dialogue in the AI clips.

Do NOT generate readable Arabic or English text, discount numerals, KIKI'S logos, watermarks, or fake signage. The real KIKI'S logo, Arabic CTA, and “50%” offer will be composited in post.
